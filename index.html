Questions

Fishing trips in Southern Utah
Where does Henrie Outfitters guide fishing trips?

Henrie Outfitters guides fishing trips at Panguitch Lake, its home water, and at Sand Hollow Reservoir, Lake Powell, Navajo Lake, Gunlock Reservoir, Quail Creek Reservoir and Fish Lake across Southern Utah.

What fish can I catch on a guided trip in Southern Utah?

Trout (rainbow, cutthroat, tiger, brook, lake trout and splake), largemouth, smallmouth and striped bass, bluegill, crappie, catfish and walleye, depending on the lake. Each lake page lists what is there.

What is included on a guided fishing trip?

A full 8 hour guided fishing trip, all fishing gear and equipment, lunch, customized start and end times to fit your schedule, and expert instruction and local knowledge. It suits beginners, families and experienced anglers.

Who is the guide?

James Henrie is the owner and guide of Henrie Outfitters LLC, a local expert in fishing and hunting with 25 years of outdoor experience.

How do I book a trip?

Call or text (435) 592-0680, email henrieoutfitters@gmail.com, or use the booking form on this page. Customized trip packages are available.
