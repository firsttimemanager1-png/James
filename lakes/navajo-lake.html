Fishing at Navajo Lake

Navajo Lake is an excellent mountain trout lake with rainbow and brook trout, in a much cooler, forested setting than the St. George area.

Guided by James Henrie, owner of Henrie Outfitters LLC. Call (435) 592-0680 or email henrieoutfitters@gmail.com to plan a trip.

What you can catch

  • Rainbow trout
  • Brook trout

Cool, forested mountain setting.

Questions about Navajo Lake

Does Henrie Outfitters guide fishing trips at Navajo Lake?

Yes. Henrie Outfitters LLC, owned and guided by James Henrie, runs guided fishing trips at Navajo Lake and at other lakes across Southern Utah.

What fish can I catch at Navajo Lake?

Navajo Lake has rainbow trout and brook trout.
